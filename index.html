How to Use FinTrack v1.6

1. Dashboard & Smart Alerts

Monitor consolidated Net Worth, asset distribution charts, monthly commitments, and receive automated reminders for Chits and 30-day Insurance Renewals.

2. Chits & Daily Pigmy Savings

Manage monthly chit funds, track taken/auctioned chits with monthly interest costs, and log daily pigmy deposits with one-tap increment buttons.

3. FDs & Insurance Portfolio

Track bank Fixed Deposits and complete Life, ULIP, Term, and Health Insurance policies with commencement dates, renewal dates, surrender values, and notes.

4. Broker Portfolios & SIPs

Record portfolio totals across brokers (AngelOne, Zerodha, Anand Rathi, Others) for Me and Spouse, along with active Mutual Fund SIP schedules.

5. Data Operations & CSV Mapping

Use the Import/Export menu to backup data to Google Drive, export CSV/JSON files, or map and import custom CSV datasets into any section.
